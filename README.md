# NZ ERA dismissal decisions — 2010–2025

Classification of public New Zealand Employment Relations Authority dismissal determinations.

![Outcome classification by year](output/charts/outcomes_by_year.png)

## Key results

- **2010–2019 strict audit:** 4,038 determinations reviewed; 2,373 substantive dismissal-merits decisions; 1,449 employee wins; 924 employer wins; **61.1% employee win rate**.
- **2020–2025 audited binary view:** 673 substantive decisions; 476 employee wins; 197 employer wins; **70.7% employee win rate**.
- **2025:** 173 substantive decisions; 119 employee wins; 54 employer wins; **68.8% employee win rate**.
- **2010–2025 combined binary view:** 3,046 substantive decisions; 1,925 employee wins; 1,121 employer wins; **63.2% employee win rate**.

There is no mixed/unclear category in the headline outcome metric. Clear legal employee/employer outcomes are preserved. For the 322 2020–2025 rows previously coded `mixed_unclear`, the tie-breaker is observable money ordered by the determination: employee-side monetary recovery minus money or costs ordered against the employee. A positive net is an employee win; zero or negative is an employer win. The original legal classification remains in the audit export.

This is an observable-order metric, not a complete accounting of private litigation economics. Legal fees or other private expenses that are not disclosed or ordered in a determination are not invented.
