Method

The distinction is the point.

A legal win and a monetary win answer different questions. Unfortunately keeps them separate at every level: headline metrics, yearly trends, case rows, and downloadable data.

01

Legal merits

Whether the Authority upheld the dismissal grievance or found the dismissal unjustified.

02

Monetary outcome

Whether the employee obtained a positive observable net monetary order in the public determination.

03

Corpus scope

The corpus is search-derived from public ERA determinations and is not described as a proven census unless recall auditing establishes that.
